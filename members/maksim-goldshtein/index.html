Bio

Originally from Russia, I am a second year Master's Student in the Smart Cities and Urban Informatics program. I have got a background in Data Analysis and social sciences. I am writing my thesis on the impacts of crises on internal migration patterns in Israeli cities. My research utilizes an agent-based urban simulation to further explore how different policies can affect those patterns in order to derive recommendations for crisis scenarios.

Department of Spatial Science, Hebrew University of Jerusalem
