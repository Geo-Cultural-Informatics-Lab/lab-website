Bio

Nimrod Korman is a master's student in geography and urban planning at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. A graduate of the Philosophy, Economics, and Political Science (PPE) program, his research takes a multidisciplinary approach to urban planning, integrating social justice, policy analysis, and resource allocation. Nimrod's thesis investigates equity in the distribution of public land uses in mixed cities, evaluating spatial accessibility to infrastructure like schools, parks, etc., for different groups in the urban space. Using a mixed-methods approach—combining spatial analysis and statistical modelling with qualitative interviews—his work examines how local planning policy shapes these socioeconomic gaps to provide a framework for future planning changes.
