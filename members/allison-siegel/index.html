Bio

"Originally from Maryland, USA and living in Israel for 5 years. I am a Research Assistant and second year Master's Student for the Smart Cities and Urban Informatics program, writing my thesis on the socioeconomic impacts of earthquakes on Israeli cities. As part of the Geo-Cultural Informatics Lab, I contribute to research that utilizes a dynamic agent based model to develop a dynamic policy-testing environment for local authorities, supporting data-driven decision making for disaster response. My independent research also utilizes an agent-based model to further explore how vulnerable populations can be supported in the face of an earthquake.
