Inbar Ben-Yair Misgav

PhD student (co-supervised with Prof. Noam Shoval)

Bio

Inbar Ben-Yair (Misgav) is a PhD candidate at the Europe Forum of the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. Her research explores the relationships between space, culture, and identity in geographical and historical contexts. She holds a BA in Geography and Anthropology and an MA in Geography and Urban Planning, both from the Hebrew University. Her master's thesis focused on spatial organization patterns in cemeteries and the ways in which they reflect processes of cultural memory, preservation, and community identity. Inbar is particularly interested in questions of collective memory, land use, and spatial representation as tools in shaping historical and social identities.
