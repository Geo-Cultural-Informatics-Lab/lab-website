Dr. Yair Grinberger

Head

Bio

Yair Grinberger is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Spatial Science at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. His fields of interest are geographical information science (GIScience), volunteered geographic information, mobility analysis, and urban modeling. Current research includes agent-based modeling of the long-term impacts of various crises, data production dynamics within OpenStreetMap, and developing geo-cultural informatic frameworks for extracting cultural information from geodata.
