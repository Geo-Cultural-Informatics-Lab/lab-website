Igal Baum

PhD student

Bio

Igal Baum is a PhD candidate in the Department of Geography at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. He holds an MA in Communication from the University of Haifa and a B.Des. in Visual Communication from Bezalel Academy of Arts and Design. His research lies at the intersection of geography, communication studies, and visual culture, focusing on the semiotic analysis of digital maps, mobile navigation applications, and platform-mediated spatial experiences at large. In his current doctoral research Igal examines how navigation apps construct spatial knowledge, user experience, and economic ideologies through dynamic (and complex) cartographic interfaces. By combining social semiotics, visual communication, and GIS, his research develops new methodological approaches for analyzing mobile geomedia and contemporary cartographic interfaces. His broader research interests include geomedia, cartographic semiotics, HCI, UX/UI, platform studies, and critical GIS. Alongside his research, Igal has extensive experience as a lecturer in visual communication, design and digital media.
