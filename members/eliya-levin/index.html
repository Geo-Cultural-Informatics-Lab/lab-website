Eliya Levin

Research Assistant

Bio

Eliya is an MA student in the Geoinformatics program, holding a BA in Geography and a BSc in Statistics and Data Science. Prior to his graduate studies, he spent two years as a member of the demographic projections group for the Jerusalem Transport Master Plan Team (JTMT), where his work focused on creating project-specific population projections and allocating employment rates using diverse data sources to support transport model inputs and calibration. His current work examines OpenStreetMap (OSM) data, with a particular focus on geospatial statistical methods for evaluating data completeness. His broader research interests lie at the intersection of geospatial data science, GIS applications, geospatial artificial intelligence (GeoAI), and the relationship between human activity and the environment. In addition to his work, Eliya is also a biking and hiking enthusiast, striving towards more green and cyclable cities.
