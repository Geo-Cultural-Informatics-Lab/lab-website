Geo-Cultural Informatics Lab

We develop geoinformatic methods to decode cultural information embedded in space, study how cultural perspectives shape spatial data, and examine how spatial information reshapes cultural and urban life.

Culture

Activities, cultural codes, communities, meanings, and social relations through which people make and organize place.

Space

Spatial forms, morphology, patterns, relations, and processes that structure cultural and urban life.

Information

GIS, maps, data, encoding and decoding, computation, and knowledge generation from spatial-cultural evidence.

Featured from the Lab

Project

A Simulation-Based Urban Dashboard for Improving Municipal Preparedness for Earthquakes

This project develops simulation-based tools for municipal earthquake preparedness, linking spatial data, urban vulnerability, and scenario analysis to support planning and decision-making before a crisis occurs.
